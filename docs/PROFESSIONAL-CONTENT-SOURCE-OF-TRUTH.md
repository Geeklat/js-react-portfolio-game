# Professional Content Source of Truth

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document governs professional content published in the portfolio, including Profile, Work, Skills, Contact, professional positioning, and résumé-related copy.

Its purpose is to keep public claims accurate, source-supported, privacy-conscious, and consistent with the repository owner's approved professional direction. It defines policy rather than inventorying the portfolio's current facts.

The portfolio may present verified material differently from its sources, but presentation must not change factual meaning or materially distort professional emphasis.

## 2. Source authority and conflicts

Use this source precedence:

1. **Master résumé — primary factual authority.** Use it for employment, titles, dates, education, technologies, responsibilities, accomplishments, metrics, skills, and other factual professional claims.
2. **Approved cover-letter or narrative material — authority for motivation, direction, and voice.** It may support Profile language and professional positioning but must not create factual work-history claims.
3. **LinkedIn — secondary consistency reference.** Use it to compare public terminology, presentation, and contact information. It does not override primary factual sources.

When sources disagree:

- retain the primary factual source unless the owner approves a correction;
- do not merge contradictory claims or silently choose convenient wording;
- surface meaningful conflicts for human review; and
- when practical, record an approved correction in the appropriate authoritative source rather than creating a portfolio-only version of the truth.

Unreviewed external material is not an approved source merely because it appears plausible or is publicly available.

## 3. Factual-integrity rules

Never invent, infer, extrapolate, or embellish:

- employers or clients;
- job titles or employment dates;
- technologies or technical skills;
- projects, responsibilities, or accomplishments;
- metrics, outcomes, business impact, or performance improvements;
- team sizes or customer names;
- project ownership, leadership authority, or decision-making scope;
- education, certifications, or awards; or
- any other professional claim presented as fact.

A technology may be presented as a skill only when supported by approved source material. Association with an employer, project, industry, or adjacent technology does not establish personal experience.

Metrics and outcomes must be explicitly supported. Do not calculate, estimate, round, strengthen, or reconstruct them from incomplete information.

If a fact is missing, ambiguous, or unsupported, leave it unresolved or surface it for human review. Do not fill gaps with likely wording, memory, placeholders resembling real facts, or generated content.

## 4. Allowed content transformations

The website does not need to reproduce source prose verbatim. Without additional approval, an agent may:

- select verified material for the portfolio;
- shorten verified statements;
- reorganize or reformat content for the interface;
- combine closely related verified material; and
- separate verified responsibilities, accomplishments, outcomes, and technologies for readability.

These transformations are allowed only when they preserve factual meaning, certainty, scope, attribution, and professional emphasis.

Human review is required before:

- strengthening or broadening a claim;
- materially changing its meaning or level of certainty;
- implying unsupported leadership, ownership, authority, or impact;
- adding unsupported technologies, metrics, outcomes, or responsibilities;
- substantially rewriting an achievement; or
- omitting, prioritizing, condensing, or reframing verified material in a way that changes the overall professional narrative.

Formatting improvements are not permission to make a claim more impressive than its source supports.

## 5. Approved professional positioning

The intended identity is an experienced, frontend-focused Lead Software Engineer with strengths in mature applications, modernization, accessibility, UX collaboration, technical planning, maintainability, mentorship, and practical AI-assisted engineering.

Broader full-stack capability may be included where verified, but it should support rather than obscure the frontend focus. Professional value should be grounded in adaptable engineering judgment rather than an exhaustive technology inventory.

AI-assisted engineering is a practical workflow capability, not the primary professional identity. Do not reposition the owner as an AI or machine-learning specialist without separately approved evidence.

Consulting and defense experience may be represented accurately, but should not eclipse the intended frontend and product-engineering narrative merely because those roles contain technically interesting material.

## 6. Privacy and public-source boundaries

Private working sources remain in ignored, untracked local storage. They are inputs to factual review, not public repository assets. Do not commit them or copy their contents into public documentation merely for convenience.

Only the reviewed facts and authored presentation intentionally selected for publication belong in tracked application data. The existence of information in AI memory, unrelated conversations, other accounts, private notes, or unreviewed external context does not authorize its use as a professional source.

Sensitive personal information—including family, health, identity, financial, relationship, or workplace-conflict details—requires explicit owner approval before public use. Professional relevance alone does not override this privacy boundary.

Do not add or change public contact methods without owner approval.

## 7. Résumé policy

Only a real, owner-supplied résumé PDF may be offered publicly.

Never generate or ship a sample, placeholder, text stand-in, substitute, reconstructed, or synthetic résumé. Do not expose a broken download while the real PDF is unavailable.

The website may select, organize, or explain verified material differently from the résumé PDF and may contain additional verified detail. Both must remain factually consistent even when their presentation and level of detail differ.

## 8. Maintenance

When professional facts, approved positioning, or source authority changes, update the appropriate authoritative source first when practical, then update this policy and portfolio data as needed.

Do not allow implementation data to become the sole authority for a professional fact. Revisit this policy when the owner's professional direction or source hierarchy materially changes.
